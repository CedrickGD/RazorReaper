# Privacy Notice

## Data Processing

RazorReaper utilizes background processes necessary for application functionality, performance optimization, and operational integrity. In the course of normal use, limited technical and environment-related data may be processed and transmitted to project-controlled infrastructure.

Such data may include:
- device- or network-derived location indicators  
- contextual accuracy and source-related metadata  
- diagnostic and operational information for analytics, security, and system reliability  

## Data Handling

Information collected through these processes is used primarily in aggregated and statistical form to support ongoing development, performance improvements, and service integrity.

- No sale or external distribution of data  
- Processing is restricted to project-operated systems  
- Retention is limited to what is necessary for operational and analytical purposes  

## Acceptance

These processes are embedded within the standard functionality of the application.
Installation, access, or continued use of RazorReaper constitutes acknowledgment of this data processing as an inherent component of the software environment.
